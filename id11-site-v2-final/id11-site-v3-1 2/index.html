What is ID11?

Football is the door.
ID11 is the network behind it.

ID11 connects players, clubs, academies, universities and professional opportunities across one global football ecosystem. Twenty years of relationships, three European hubs, and a degree awarded by ISC Paris Business School running alongside the football — so no sixteen-year-old has to gamble their whole future on one trial.

The pathway

From potential
to possibility.

Six stages. Each one is a real decision point with a real person on the other side of it — and you can stop, change direction or take the education route at any of them.

See the full pathway

01

Assess

An honest read on where you are today — level, position, profile, and the routes that are realistically open to you.

02

Develop

Training inside an ID11 hub in Paris, Málaga or Cologne. Daily coaching, a real competitive calendar, and a plan you can see.

03

Be seen

Talent days, showcases and club sessions. Your profile goes to the people who actually make decisions.

04

Get placed

Trials, contracts and transfers through relationships built over two decades — not cold emails.

05

Study

A degree alongside the football, so the years count for something whichever way the game goes.

06

Build your future

A network, a qualification and a career — in football or beyond it. That is the point of the eleventh player.

Study & Play

Don’t choose between football and your future.

Train in Europe and read for a degree at the same time. The academic side is awarded by ISC Paris Business School, accredited by AACSB and AMBA. If football goes the way you want it to, you have both. If it doesn’t, you still have one.
